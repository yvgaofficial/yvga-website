About YVGA

A Global Platform For Youth Voices

YVGA brings together emerging leaders, students, professionals, innovators and changemakers around shared ideas, international understanding and constructive global engagement.

Youth Leadership

Encouraging young people to develop confidence, leadership capabilities and a responsible approach to global challenges.

International Dialogue

Creating opportunities for young voices to exchange perspectives and engage in respectful cross-cultural and international dialogue.

Global Collaboration

Building connections between young people from different backgrounds to encourage cooperation, ideas and future youth-led initiatives.
